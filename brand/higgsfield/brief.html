Higgsfield brief: head-to-toe kits on a real runner

How to turn the six OFFNORM kits into photo-real model shots on Higgsfield: which files to upload, in what order, and the exact prompts. Open the zip OFFNORM-Higgsfield-pack.zip alongside this page.

What's in the pack

FileWhat it isUpload?
_00_full_kit.pngWhole outfit laid flat, 1100×1820 PNGYes, always first
_01_beanie to _06_long_socks.pngOne garment per image, 2048×2048 PNG, plain light-grey background, no textYes, as garment references
_99_board.pngLabelled board with colour names and hex codesNo. Use it to check results
_prompts.txtNine ready prompts per kit plus the negative promptCopy and paste
logo/Transparent PNGs of the OFN mark, OFN vertical, symbol and wordmark, black and whiteFor fixing logos after generation

Workflow

  1. Lock one model first. Generate a single runner, or train a Soul ID from 20+ photos of a real person who has agreed to it. Reuse that face and body as a reference in every kit so the lookbook is consistent. Don't use photos of other brands' athletes.
  2. Pick a model that takes several reference images. Soul 2.0 is strong for realism and fashion mood; a multi-reference model keeps the garments closer to the drawings. Try both on one kit and keep the better one. Check each model's upload limit in the app.
  3. Upload the references in the order below, plus the locked model image.
  4. Paste the prompt for the shot, and the negative prompt if the model has a field for it.
  5. Generate 4 at a time, pick the closest, then change one thing in the prompt at a time.
  6. Check against the board using the checklist below.
  7. Fix logos afterwards. AI models usually distort logos and small text. Use the edit/inpaint tool on the logo, or place the transparent PNG from logo/ in Photoshop or Canva, warped to the fabric.
  8. Video, optional. Take the best still into image-to-video: "the runner jogs slowly toward the camera, studio light, camera static, 5 seconds".

Upload order

If the model limits the number of references, stop where it stops.

  • 1_00_full_kit
  • 2_02_tee_front
  • 3_05_short_tights
  • 4_06_long_socks
  • 5_01_beanie
  • 6_03_tee_back (back shot only)
  • 7_04_arm_sleeve
