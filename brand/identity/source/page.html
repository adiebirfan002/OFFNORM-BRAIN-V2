The idea

Norm: off.

The first O is a switch, and the switch is set to off.

OFFNORM has two O's. The one in OFF is off. The one in NORM stays normal. You read the brand name and see what it means at the same time. Alone, the O is the symbol. With FN riding in its track, it becomes the badge.
