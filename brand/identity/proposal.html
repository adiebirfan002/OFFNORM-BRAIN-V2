The idea

One is off.

Nine dots sit in a grid. One has left it. That dot is the brand.

It is the runner who is not on pace. The run that goes a bit wrong. A cotton tee in a polyester sport. A Malaysian label in a category designed for cold countries. Every piece of the identity is built from that one move: keep everything in order, then let one thing be off.

Narrative
Running tells you there is a right way to do it. Right pace, right shoes, right weather, right shirt. We run in Malaysia. It is 27 degrees before the sun is up. The air is wet. It rains at four. Most runs here go a bit wrong, and people go out anyway. OFFNORM makes running clothes for those runs. Cotton, cut properly, built for the heat. Not for the perfect run. Nobody has had one. What the brand stands on

Satisfy owns the high. ACG owns all conditions. norda owns a fibre. OFFNORM takes the word already inside its name. A runner has an off day far more often than a runner's high, so there is always something true to say.

One condition. Cotton holds sweat. The story only works if the fabric really runs in KL heat: light open knit, loose cut, fast to dry. If the sample fails a real 10 km, the line softens to "run in it, live in it" and the page changes with it.

Logo systemEvery letter drawn, none typed

Eight marks, one family

Wide, heavy capitals with a flat-sided O. No tricks inside any letter. The character is in the proportion and in one shared rule: the bars sit low.

The rules behind it

Low bars

The bar of the F and the waist of the R sit below the centre line. One rule, shared by every letter, so the word looks slightly off without any single letter being a trick.

3° off level

Kuala Lumpur sits at 3° north. Every sewn label, tab and sticker is set 3° off level. The master logo itself stays straight.
