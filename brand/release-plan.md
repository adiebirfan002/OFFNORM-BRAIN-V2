# OFFNORM release plan 2027 (proposal)

**Status: proposal by Mr. Branding (OF-02), 2026-11-26. Nothing here is confirmed.**

## The founder's plan (as given)

- **Early 2027:** ready-made "startup" pieces to introduce the brand to Malaysians.
- **Limited editions:** May, July, September and November 2027.
- Two options for each limited drop:
  1. **Three collections per drop** (e.g. The Wild v1, The Moss v1, The Dune v1), 7 pieces each: bandana, running cap,
     shirt, running shirt, shorts, tights, socks. That's 21 designs per drop.
  2. **One collection per drop**, the same 7 pieces. That's 7 designs per drop, one theme, one mood.
- Open question: should there also be a permanent range?

## What the reference brands do

| Brand | Permanent core | Limited / seasonal |
|---|---|---|
| Satisfy | Signature staples (MothTech tees, CloudMerino) stay; each season refreshes them with new graphics and colours | Themed capsules on a set date, some split into numbered parts (Winter Pack drop 1, drop 2) |
| Tracksmith | Named core franchises (Van Cortlandt, Strata, Team Issued), its first product still sold | Yearly limited capsules, race editions for major marathons, members get early access |
| Ciele | The GoCap in standard colours, always in stock | Limited colourways, city editions (Boston), one-time collabs, anniversary capsule |
| Bandit | Seasonal lines that grow each year, started from one product (socks) | Race-weekend capsules (e.g. a 10-piece Boston capsule, NYC "Run City"), membership drives repeat buying |
| FPA | Plain core range sold in its stores and online | Capsules built like team kits, numbered |
| norda | Numbered models (001, 002) | Limited colourways and collabs on the same models |

**The pattern: every one of them has a permanent core and puts limited drops on top of it.** The core pays the bills
and gets reordered. The limited drops carry the story and the hype, and send people back to the core. Nobody we
studied runs only on limited drops.

DTC drop economics: drops sell through at full price more often, but the cash sits in stock for months. The advice
across sources is a **small, restockable core plus drops sold in small runs or on pre-order**. Socks, tees and caps
carry the least stock risk.

## Recommendation

### 1. Pick option 2: one collection per drop
- **One story per drop.** The collection idea (one Malaysian place plus its weather) only lands if each drop gets the
  whole spotlight. Three at once means three stories competing in one launch post.
- **A third of the work and stock risk.** 7 designs a drop instead of 21. Across four drops that's 28 designs a year
  instead of 84, before sizes and colours. Factory minimums usually apply per style and colour, so 21 designs can
  multiply stock fast (check the real minimums with the maker).
- **The line-up lasts longer.** Seven places, one per drop, gives almost two years of drops. Three per drop burns
  through them in under a year.
- **Each drop is a moment.** One name, one date, one countdown. Easier for content, run-club events and press.

### 2. Make the early-2027 launch the permanent core, not random ready-made pieces
Call it something like **OFFNORM Daily** or **Core**: 3-4 pieces in 2-3 calm colours, always restocked. For example:
running shirt, short tights, socks, cap. Same logo system, no collection theme.
- It gives new customers something to buy between drops.
- It's where the reorders and cash flow come from.
- If "ready-made" means stock blanks with our logo, make sure they still look like OFFNORM. A generic first
  product is the hardest first impression to undo.

### 3. Map the four 2027 drops to the weather
Founder's confirmed order (2026-11-12) is Sky + Dune, Ocean, Monsoon, Moss + Wild, Caves. With one collection per
drop, a suggested fit:

| Drop | Month | Collection | Why then |
|---|---|---|---|
| 001 | May 2027 | The Dune | Hottest, driest stretch; dry-heat kit |
| 002 | July 2027 | The Sky | Mid-year, clear-sky season |
| 003 | September 2027 | The Ocean | Last calm sea before the monsoon |
| 004 | November 2027 | The Monsoon | The monsoon starts on the east coast. Perfect timing |

The Moss, The Wild and The Caves move to 2028. **This changes the confirmed pairing (Sky + Dune together), so the
founder needs to approve it.**

### 4. Number and return, don't just "version"
Instead of "The Wild v1 / 2.0", number every drop like an archive (FPA, norda): **DROP 001 · THE DUNE**. Then let
a collection **return** on its season, e.g. The Monsoon comes back every November as **THE MONSOON 02**. Returning
collections build a yearly ritual people wait for.

### 5. Not all 7 pieces every time
Keep a fixed kit (running shirt, short tights, socks, cap) and rotate the rest (bandana, shirt, shorts) only when
they fit the place. A bandana makes sense for The Dune and The Monsoon; it's less useful for The Sky. Fewer pieces,
each with a reason.

## Risks to check

- **Four drops in seven months is tight** for design, sampling and production. Each drop needs its own lead time
  (often 2-3 months from approved sample to stock). Confirm with the maker before locking the dates. Three drops in
  2027 is a safe fallback.
- **Small runs.** Keep first-drop quantities small and sell out. A sold-out drop builds the brand; leftover limited
  stock on discount damages it.
- **Pre-orders or a waitlist** for limited drops cut cash risk.
