# OFFNORM colourways (proposal)

**Status: proposal, awaiting founder's picks.** 20 colourways on the approved kit (tee, cap, shorts with 3/4
tights, long socks). Only colours change; the designs are the approved ones in `brand/garments`.

Each colourway is five colours with fixed jobs: Body (tee, tights), Panel (right-sleeve panel, short hip panel),
Thread (seams, drawcord, tab, sock band), Deep (short, dark sock), Light (cap crown, light sock).

| Family | Colourways |
|---|---|
| Makan | 01 Teh Tarik, 02 Kopi O, 03 Sambal, 04 Pandan, 05 Durian, 06 Kuih Lapis |
| Kain | 07 Batik Indigo, 08 Songket, 09 Pasar Malam, 10 Kampung, 11 Tanah Merah |
| Langit | 12 Subuh, 13 Hujan Petang, 14 Senja, 15 Bulan |
| Alam | 16 Kinabalu, 17 Kabus Genting, 18 Laut Sabah, 19 Bunga Kertas, 20 Paya Bakau |

Hex values are screen colours; fabric needs lab dips before anything is final.

Rebuild: `python3 brand/colourways/source/build.py`
