Colours that remember.
ProposalSixteen vintage colourways on the approved tee, cap, shorts with 3/4 tights and long socks, grouped by era. Nothing in the design changes, only the colours and the finish. Pick the ones you want to keep.
The idea

Malaysia, a few decades ago

Each colourway is taken from something people grew up with here: the pink minibus, the kopitiam floor, the school uniform, the biscuit tin on the shelf.

The colours are softer and warmer than the first twenty, as if washed many times and left in the sun. The same five-colour rule applies: Body, Panel, Thread, Deep and Light each have a fixed place on the kit.

Vintage finishProposed, for every piece
  • Pigment washGarment-dyed after sewing, so seams and edges fade slightly lighter than the body.
  • Soft printLogo and wordmark in a water-based print that softens and cracks a little with wear, in cream rather than pure white.
  • Tonal stitchingSeams sewn in the Thread colour as before, but slightly duller, so they look aged rather than new.
  • Aged labelsWoven labels on an off-white ground instead of bright white.

Drawings below have a light wash applied to suggest the finish; swatches show the true hex. Fabric needs lab dips and wash tests before anything is final.
