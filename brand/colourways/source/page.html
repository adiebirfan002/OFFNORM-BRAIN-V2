How they are built

Five colours, one rule

Every colourway is five colours with fixed jobs, the same system the seven collections already use:

  • BodyThe tee, and the tights.
  • PanelThe right-sleeve panel and the short's hip panel.
  • ThreadEvery contrast seam, the drawcord, the hem tab, the sock band.
  • DeepThe short, and the dark sock.
  • LightThe cap crown, and the light sock.

Grouped into four families so they can drop as small capsules: Makan, Kain, Langit and Alam.

Every name is Malaysian, in keeping with the collections. The colours are picked from the thing they are named after, so the story comes with the colourway.

Hex values are screen colours. Fabric dyes will need lab dips from the factory before anything is final.
