OFFNORM · Long sleeve · Draft 1

The tee, long sleeve

The approved tee taken to the wrist, in the six head-to-toe colourways. Same body, same raglan seams, same logo placement; only the sleeves change.

  • Sleeves to the wrist, with a rib cuff. The raglan seams in the pop colour run from the neck to the underarm, as on the tee.
  • The panel stays on the wearer's right arm only. It starts at the same point on the raglan seam and runs down the outer forearm to the cuff, so every seam still meets another seam.
  • Front: symbol on the chest, small tab on the hem. Back: wordmark and data block, F and NM at the hem corners. The number reads OFN—code—LS1.
  • With the long sleeve, the arm sleeve is no longer needed. In the head-to-toe kit it replaces the tee and arm sleeve together.
