OFFNORM · Long sleeve · Draft 2

The tee, long sleeve

Redrawn from your FPA long-sleeve reference, in the six head-to-toe colourways. Taken from FPA: the relaxed boxy fit, the long straight sleeves, the contrast-stitched panels and the mesh for air. Made OFFNORM: the panels on both sides are cut the same, but only the wearer's right side is coloured, and every panel seam starts from one point on the raglan seam.

  • Fit: relaxed and boxy, long straight sleeves with a plain stitched hem, no rib cuff. Like the reference.
  • Panels: on each side, one panel runs down the outer sleeve to the cuff and one runs down the side of the body to the hem. Both start at the same point on the raglan seam, marked with a small reflective dot.
  • Colour: the wearer's right-side panels are in the panel colour; the left-side panels stay in the body colour, cut in mesh and outlined by the stitching. The asymmetry from the tee carries on.
  • Stitching: every panel seam and both raglan seams are contrast overlock in the pop colour, like the reference's visible seams.
  • Mesh: all four panels are in an open mesh knit for air, which suits Malaysian heat.
  • Logos: symbol on the chest and the hem tab on the front; wordmark, data block, F and NM on the back. Not copied from FPA: their chest text, triangle badge, big back logo, reflective dashes and the bandana.
  • With the long sleeve, the arm sleeve is not needed. In the kit it replaces the tee and arm sleeve together.
