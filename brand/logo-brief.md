# OFFNORM logo brief

**Status: proposal.** This is a brief, not a logo. No marks are drawn in Brain V2 yet. Logo rounds v1 to v11 and
their files stay in Brain V1 (private repo OFFNORM-BRAIN).

## What the four references teach about logos

| Brand | Mark | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| ACG | Three letters in a fixed container | Container is recognised before the letters are read. Any colour, any size |
| FPA | Three-letter block plus small text | Acronym re-expands into slogans. Reads as team or sponsor mark |
| Satisfy | Plain caps wordmark | Does nothing clever. Labels, slogans and fabric names carry the character |
| norda | Lowercase wordmark with ™ | Restraint. The legal mark is part of the drawing |

Shared rules:

1. Letters only. No runner, no wing, no speed lines.
2. One fixed thing (a shape, a case, a symbol) that never changes.
3. A system of small supporting pieces around it: numbers, ®/™, labels, lines of small text.
4. Works in one colour.
5. Reads at cap-embroidery size.

## What the founder has already ruled out (from V1)

- Tricks inside the letter O (v1 to v8, and the forward O).
- Anything typed in a stock font (v9, v10).
- Plain wordmark plus ≠ sign (v9, passed over).
- Stencil, tapir, split Off/Norm (v10, passed over).

Still on the table from V1: **v11**, the OFN / OFF system with letters drawn as geometry (sign, OFN ®,
OFF/NORM box, OFFNORM ® wordmark). No verdict yet.

## The brief

**Build a two-part system: a short mark and a wordmark.**

| Part | Job | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| Short mark: **OFF** or **OFN** | Chest hit, cap, sock, hang tag, avatar | ACG, FPA |
| Wordmark: **OFFNORM** | Neck label, site header, packaging, back print | Satisfy, norda |
| Support set | ®, product number, "conditions" label, coordinates line | all four |

### OFF or OFN?

| | OFF | OFN |
|---|---|---|
| Reads as | A real word, instantly | An acronym, needs learning |
| Fits the narrative | Directly: the owned word in [narrative.md](narrative.md) is "off" | Indirectly |
| Ownable | Weak. Off-White, OFF! and others sit close; hard to trademark alone | Strong. Unusual letter set |
| Copy play | Off day, off pace, off road | Re-expansions, as FPA does |
| Letter shapes | O-F-F: repeat of F gives rhythm | O-F-N: three different widths, harder to balance |

**Recommendation: OFN is the registered mark, "off" is the word used in copy.** The logo stays ownable and the
language stays human. If the founder prefers OFF on product, always lock it to NORM or to the container so it is
never a lone "OFF".

### The container

ACG's lesson is that the container does the recognising. A triangle is taken. Requirements for OFFNORM's:

- Not a triangle, not a circle with letters on a curve, not a shield.
- Should mean something to the narrative. Directions worth sketching:
  - **Off-register box**: a rectangle whose frame and fill are printed slightly out of line, like a misprinted
    label. Says "off" without a word. Simple to embroider as two offset shapes.
  - **Tilted label**: the three letters in a plain box set a few degrees off level. One rule: it is always off by
    the same angle. (Pick the angle and make it a brand fact, for example Kuala Lumpur's latitude, 3°.)
  - **Road sign**, carried from v11: the end-of-restriction sign. Keep only if it still reads at 10 mm.
- Test each at 10 mm wide in one colour before judging it at poster size.

### The letters

- Drawn, not typed. Continue from the geometric letters in V1's `kit.py` if the founder likes their feel.
- Heavy enough to embroider: no stroke thinner than about 1 mm at cap size.
- One deliberate "off" detail across the whole alphabet, not a trick in a single letter. Examples: every
  horizontal bar sits slightly below centre; every terminal is cut at the same 3° angle. Subtle enough that it
  feels like character and not a gimmick.

### Colour

Black and white first (already the V1 rule). Add colour the ACG way: taken from the place, named after the
place. Starting swatches to sample from photographs, not from a chart: laterite red soil, monsoon grey, wet
tarmac, palm green, 6am sky, teh tarik. One accent per drop.

### Support set (where the personality lives)

- **®** on the short mark from day one, as part of the drawing. (Use ™ until registration is granted. This is a
  legal point: check with MyIPO or a trademark agent before printing ®.)
- **Product number**: OFN-001.
- **Conditions label**: a small woven label stating the conditions the piece was made for. "31°C / 84% / 0530".
- **Origin line**: "Designed at 3° N" and the true place of manufacture.
- **Care label copy** in brand voice.

## Tests every candidate must pass

1. 10 mm wide, one colour, embroidered: still readable?
2. 16 px avatar: still recognisable?
3. Covered name test: without the word OFFNORM beside it, does it look like ACG, FPA, Off-White or a road
   authority? If yes, redraw.
4. Reversed (white on black) with no redrawing.
5. On a washed cotton tee, a cap, a sock cuff and a hang tag, in a mock-up.
6. Does it still look right next to a joke on a care label?

## Next step

Founder picks: OFN or OFF, and one container direction. Then draw three candidates only, run the six tests, and
show them on product, not on a white board.
