The Sky tee, approved

Your last amendment is applied without a further round, as you asked: the white lower-sleeve panel is on the right arm only, and the back uses the D3 layout. This flat is now the master for every collection.

  1. Right arm only. One white lower-sleeve panel on the wearer's right arm, joined to the raglan seam at a bar tack. The left sleeve is plain.
  2. Both raglans stay in the contrast thread, each ending in a reflective dot at the underarm.
  3. Front: the O in the middle of the chest, and the hem tab.
  4. Back: wordmark, the D3 block, and the F and NM at the hem corners.
  5. For each collection only three things change: body, panel and thread colour; the product code and name; the coordinate and conditions.

Cap, shorts with 3/4 tights, long socks

Same rules as the tee: one contrast panel on the wearer's right side only, the collection's thread on the seams around it, a bar tack where seams meet, the O small, and the coordinate in small type. The socks carry OFFNORM set vertically up the leg.

The colours are played differently on each piece. The tee keeps each collection's main colour, so the cap, the short, the tights and the socks each take another pairing from the same five-colour palette. Worn together they read as one kit without matching.

{{KIT}}
  1. Cap. Five-panel. The O on the front panel, a contrast panel on the wearer's right side, the brim in that same contrast colour with the coordinate on its edge.
  2. Shorts. A loose 5-inch short with a drawcord in the thread colour, a contrast right hip panel, a split hem and a small tab at the waistband.
  3. 3/4 tights. Built into the short, in a third colour, ending below the knee. One thread-colour seam runs down the right leg with the coordinate; a small O sits above the left hem.
  4. Long socks. Two colourways per collection, a light pair and a dark pair. The wordmark reads bottom to top; heel and toe take a second colour.
