The Sky panel tee

Pale sky blue with one white panel and sun-yellow thread. The seam lines are the design; the print stays small on the front and large on the back.

  1. The slash. One double seam crosses the body at the same angle as the cut in your logo. This replaces FPA's curved frame.
  2. One raglan only. A contrast raglan seam and white sleeve panel on one side. The other shoulder is plain.
  3. Seams that miss. The two hem seams sit about a centimetre apart where they meet the slash, on purpose.
  4. Small chest mark. The O at about 3 cm, with the product number under it.
  5. Coordinate on the seam. The coordinate runs along the slash in small type, where FPA would put a slogan.
  6. Reflective dots at every seam end.
  7. Hem tab in thread yellow, sewn 3° off level.
  8. Mirrored on the back. The slash and the single panel continue round to the other side.
  9. The O in a sun ring. The Sky's dot graphic circles the O above the slash, with one dot off.
  10. The F and the NM sit below the slash, so the seam splits the logo into two zones.
  11. Captions are data. The conditions under the O, latitude under the F, longitude under the NM.
