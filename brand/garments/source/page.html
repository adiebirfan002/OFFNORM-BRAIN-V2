Close-upThe sleeve you flagged

How the sleeve panel is built

In round 2 the yellow raglan stitch and the white panel did not touch. Here the panel is cut as part of the sleeve, so its seam starts on the raglan seam and every line ends at a point.

  1. Raglan seam. Runs from the neck rib to the underarm in one line of yellow cover stitch.
  2. The junction. Halfway down the raglan, the panel seam branches off. A bar tack in the same thread locks the point where the two seams meet.
  3. Panel seam. Curves from the junction to the sleeve hem, dividing the sleeve into a blue upper and a white lower.
  4. Underarm point. The raglan, the side seam and the bottom of the panel all end here, marked with a reflective dot.
  5. Sleeve hem. Turned and stitched, running across both colours in one line.
  6. Logo. The O sits in the middle of the white panel, square to the arm, clear of every seam.
