The Sky tee, three directions

Round 1, the single slash across the body, was rejected. You asked for the logo in the middle or on the arm, and a minimal design that still shows the identity. These are three ways to do that, in the same colours. The cut is now boxier and closer to your reference.

Direction ACentreA plain tee. Two yellow raglan seams, one hem seam that breaks and misses, and the O small in the middle of the chest. The wordmark sits high on the back.
Direction BArmNothing on the chest at all. One white sleeve carries the O. The F and the NM sit small at the two back hem corners.
Direction CThe quiet OOne colour. The panel lines on the body trace your O at full size in tone-on-tone thread, so the identity is in the build. The O itself is small on the arm.
Your approvalPick A, B or C, or mix them (for example the seams of B with the back of A). If none is right, tell me one thing you dislike and one FPA piece you want it closer to.
