The idea

Every collection is a coordinate

Each collection is named after one place in Malaysia and built for the weather at that exact point.

The coordinate goes on the garment, on the conditions label and on the back print. The colours are taken from the place. The fabric weight follows the weather there. A runner can go to the coordinate and find the conditions the piece was made for.

This also widens the brand story. Round 1 said hot and wet. Your list is truer: Malaysia is cold mist at 2,000 metres, dry sand in Melaka, a calm sea in July and a wild one in December. No single condition is the norm.

The seven coordinates plotted by latitude and longitude, with Kuala Lumpur marked +. Points only, no coastline is drawn. Six are on the peninsula, The Caves is in Sarawak.

The system

One kit, seven places

Every collection carries the same five core pieces, so the pattern-making is done once and only colour, fabric weight and the back print change. Each collection then adds one or two pieces that only make sense in that place.

Numbering

OFN—MOS—001

Brand, collection, piece. The number is printed under the chest mark and on the hang tag.

The conditions label

Each piece has a woven label with the temperature, humidity and time of day at its coordinate, sewn 3° off level. The figures on this page are estimates. The real ones should be logged on site, which is also the first shoot for each collection.

The back print

Always dots, always one colour, always with the collection name and coordinate under it. Moss clusters, a river, a sun ring, waves, stalactites, sand ripples, sideways rain.

The order, confirmed

Seven collections at once is a lot of stock for a new brand. Releasing one or two at a time keeps each drop small and gives the brand something new to say five times.

Drop 1The Sky + The DuneHot, lowland, close to KL and Melaka. This is the weather most of your runners train in, and the two cheapest places to shoot.
Drop 2The OceanThe calm sea, for the dry months on the east coast.
Drop 3The MonsoonThe same sea in the other season. Released for the northeast monsoon, November to March.
Drop 4The Moss + The WildThe cold pair. Heavier fabrics and long sleeves, the highest price points.
Drop 5The CavesThe night-running collection, and the first one from Borneo.

Things to settle

  • Moss and Wild are closeBoth are cold, misty and green in your notes. Here they are split: Moss is still and green, Wild is moving, night black and river brown with an orange seam.
  • Sky and Ocean are closeBoth are blue and warm. Here Sky is pale blue and white for air, Ocean is teal and sand with coral for water.
  • Three places, confirmedYou confirmed Sky Mirror in Kuala Selangor for The Sky, Cherating in Pahang for The Monsoon, and the Gunung Tahan summit for The Wild.
  • Cotton in the cold and the wetThe Moss, The Wild and The Monsoon need warmth or rain cover. The shells cannot be cotton, and the brushed long sleeves need a fabric test like the tee does.
  • Exact coordinatesThe points come from public sources and differ by a few hundred metres between them. Before anything is printed, stand on the spot and take the reading yourselves.
  • The logoMarks on this page are plain. Once you choose the O and the emblem, every tee here updates.
