{{HERO}}
Seven locals. One country.
ProposalBuilt from your list of seven Malaysian animals and flowers. You named no places, so all seven habitats are my picks. Colours, pieces and order are suggestions.
The idea

Every collection is something that lives here

Line-up 01 was seven places. Line-up 02 is seven living things that belong to Malaysia, each pinned to a coordinate where it can still be found.

The colours come from the animal or the flower itself. The back print draws it in dots. Each one also lends the collection a job: the hornbill is the be-seen kit, the turtle is for long slow runs, the Rafflesia is for rest days. That keeps seven collections from being seven colourways of the same tee.

{{CHART}}

The seven habitats plotted by latitude and longitude, with Kuala Lumpur marked +. Points only, no coastline is drawn. Three are on the peninsula, one in Sarawak, three in Sabah.

{{BANDS}}
The system

Same kit, same rules as line-up 01

The five core pieces, the numbering, the conditions label and the dotted back print all carry over, so the two line-ups read as one brand. Only the subject changes, from a place to a living thing.

CollectionCodeCoordinateHabitatPieces only this one gets

One job each

Enggang: be seen. Harimau: dusk. Rafflesia: rest day. Bunga Raya: race day. Penyu: long and slow. Beruang Matahari: short and hard. Gajah Pygmy: the group run.

Give something back

Five of the seven are protected or threatened. A brand that sells a tiger should fund tigers. Each collection should name one conservation partner and a fixed share per piece. This needs real agreements before any of it is announced.

Names stay in Malay

Enggang, Harimau, Penyu. Short, local, and no other running brand has them. The Latin name and status go on the hang tag.

A suggested order

Five drops again, starting with the one every Malaysian already knows.

Drop 1Bunga RayaThe national flower, for Merdeka on 31 August and Malaysia Day on 16 September. The easiest story to tell first.
Drop 2Harimau + EnggangThe two best-known animals. Dusk and dawn, orange on black and black with orange.
Drop 3PenyuThe long-run collection, on its own so the slower story has room.
Drop 4Beruang Matahari + Gajah PygmyThe Sabah pair. The smallest bear and the smallest elephant.
Drop 5RafflesiaSeven days on sale, then gone until it blooms again.

Things to settle

  • All seven places are my picksYou gave species, not locations. Each coordinate is a park or sanctuary where it is found. Penyu could move to Terengganu if you want it near The Ocean.
  • How the two line-ups run togetherFourteen collections is two to three years of drops. Either finish line-up 01 first, or alternate one place with one living thing.
  • Harimau is crowded groundHarimau Malaya is also the national football team, and tiger stripes are on many jerseys. Keep this one dark and quiet so it does not read as a football shirt.
  • Check the facts with a partnerPopulation figures and statuses here come from news and conservation sources and change over time. A conservation partner should confirm them before they are printed.
  • Rafflesia spelling and speciesIt is spelled Rafflesia, with two f's. Malaysia has several species; the one at Gunung Gading is Rafflesia tuan-mudae.
  • Estimates and the logoWeather figures are estimates, coordinates are park-level points from public sources, and the logo is plain until the O is chosen.
