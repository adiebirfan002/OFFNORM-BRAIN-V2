# OFFNORM narrative

**Status: proposal. Nothing here is confirmed until the founder says so.**
Built from [../research/patterns.md](../research/patterns.md). Carried over from Brain V1 as confirmed: the brand
is Malaysian, cotton-led, fashion-forward, for running, and the belief line is **"No run is perfect."**

The visual side of this proposal is in [identity/](identity/README.md): idea "one is off", the Stray symbol,
Bandung pink as the one accent.

## Which norm?

The name asks a question the brand has to answer. Three possible answers, each a different brand:

| | A. Off the perfect | B. Off the climate | C. Off the uniform |
|---|---|---|---|
| The norm | The perfect run: the split, the PB, the photo | Running kit and running culture made for cold countries | Looking like every other runner: neon polyester, race tee |
| OFFNORM says | The off day counts | We run at the equator, at 5am, in 90% humidity | Run in what you would wear anyway |
| Learns from | Satisfy (anti-performance) | ACG (conditions), norda (place) | FPA ("performance without the uniform") |
| Risk | Close to Satisfy if said romantically | Cotton has to really work in the heat | Becomes a fashion brand that mentions running |

**Recommendation: A is the belief, B is the place, cotton is the proof.** A alone is a mood. B alone is a
weather report. Together: nobody runs a perfect run here, the conditions make sure of it, and that is the point.
C falls out for free once the clothes exist.

## The frame

| Piece | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Enemy | The perfect run. Perfect pace, perfect weather, perfect feed |
| Belief | No run is perfect. *(confirmed)* |
| Owned word | **Off.** Off day, off pace, off road, off hours, off season, off the norm |
| Place | The equator. Hot, wet, dark at both ends of the day |
| Proof | Cotton that is built to be run in here (see "Product truth" below) |
| People | **Off Day Club**: runners who are not only runners, and not only fast |
| Tone | Dry, funny, honest. Serious about the clothes, never about ourselves |

Why "off" as the owned word: it is already half the name, it is the three-letter mark the founder asked for, it
is ordinary speech in Malaysia ("off day", "off sikit"), and it turns every bad run into brand material. Satisfy
sells the high. OFFNORM can sell the off day, which every runner has far more often.

## The story, short

> Running tells you there is a right way to do it. Right pace, right shoes, right weather, right shirt.
>
> We run in Malaysia. It is 27 degrees before the sun is up. The air is wet. It rains at four. Most runs here go
> a bit wrong, and people go out anyway, before work, after work, in between.
>
> OFFNORM makes running clothes for those runs. Cotton, cut properly, built for the heat. Not for the perfect
> run. Nobody has had one.
>
> No run is perfect.

## The story, one line

- Running clothes for the off days. Which is most of them.
- Made at the equator for runs that go a bit wrong.
- Off pace. Off day. Off norm.

## Product truth (must be solved before the story is told)

A cotton running brand in the tropics invites one question: *doesn't cotton hold sweat?* It does. norda did not
launch until its material was right, and Satisfy made cotton work by engineering it. OFFNORM needs its own
answer, and it needs a name. Options to test with a manufacturer:

- Very light open-knit or mesh-knit cotton (well under 150 gsm) so it holds less water and dries faster.
- Cotton blended with lyocell or a little recycled poly for drying speed, still cotton to the hand.
- Loose boxy cut with real air gap, side vents, raw or bonded hems that do not hold water.
- Garment-washed so it is soft from run one and already "imperfect".

Give the chosen fabric one name and use it everywhere, the way Satisfy uses MothTech and norda uses Dyneema.
The name should come from the founder once the fabric exists; do not invent it before there is a sample.

**If the fabric cannot be made to work for a real 10 km in KL heat, the honest position is "run in it, live in
it" (answer C) and the story above should be softened. Do not claim performance the shirt cannot deliver.**

## Voice

ACG is the model for attitude, Malaysia is the model for language.

- Short sentences. Plain words. One joke at most, and never at the runner's expense.
- English first with rojak where it is natural, never forced. Decide the BM/EN mix with the founder.
- Admit things. "It will get heavy if it rains. So will you."
- Never: motivational quotes, "crush", "beast mode", "no excuses", pace as a virtue.

Sample lines (drafts):

- Off day? Run anyway.
- 6:45 pace. Still counts.
- Walked the last 2K. Listed as a run.
- Designed at 3° north.
- Not tested in a wind tunnel. Tested on Jalan Ampang at 6am.
- Care label: wash cold. Run hot.

## Decisions the founder owes

Carried from the V1 brand guide, with a recommendation for each so they can be answered quickly:

1. **Which norm?** Recommended: A + B above.
2. **Road, trail or both?** Recommended: road and city first. That is where Malaysian runners are.
3. **Target runner?** Recommended: 22 to 35, runs with a club or alone, cares about clothes, not chasing podiums.
4. **Price tier?** Recommended: above local sports-shop price, well below Satisfy. A tee people can buy twice.
5. **Loud or quiet?** Recommended: quiet clothes, loud copy.
6. **Language?** Recommended: English with light rojak.
7. **Never look like?** Recommended: a marathon finisher tee; a Satisfy copy; a hype streetwear drop.
