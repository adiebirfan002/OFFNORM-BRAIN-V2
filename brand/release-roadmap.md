# OFFNORM 2027 roadmap (proposal)

**Status: proposal by Mr. Branding (OF-02), 2026-11-26. Builds on [release-plan.md](release-plan.md). Nothing confirmed.**

## 1. How long one drop takes

Planning figures (confirm with the real maker; sources vary):

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Design + tech packs | ~4 weeks |
| Sampling (2-3 rounds) | ~4-8 weeks |
| Bulk production, QC, delivery | ~6-12 weeks |
| Shoot + content with real stock | ~2 weeks before launch |
| **Total, design start to launch** | **~5 months** |

So with a drop every two months, **two or three drops are always in progress at the same time**: one being
designed, one in sampling, one in production.

### How the permanent collection runs

- **Always on sale.** Kit 000 never sells out on purpose. It's what people buy between drops.
- **Start with 4 pieces in 3 colours:** for example running shirt, short tights, socks and cap. That's 12 products
  before sizes.
- **Reorder rule.** A repeat order is faster than a first order, because the pattern is already approved (plan on
  about 6-8 weeks; confirm with the maker). Reorder a size and colour when about 40% of its stock is left, so it
  lands before it runs out.
- **Grow slowly: one new permanent piece per year.** The first one (e.g. a long sleeve or a bandana) is added in
  August, chosen from what customers asked for and what sold best in the drops.
- **Promote from the drops.** If a drop piece sells out fast and people keep asking, it can join Kit 000 in a
  permanent colour. The limited colourway stays limited.
- **No discounts, no seasonal colour churn.** Colours stay fixed for the year so restocks stay simple. New colours
  belong to the drops.
- **Plan 2028 in October.** Fabric upgrades, a fourth colour or a new piece are decided at the year review, so the
  first 2028 order can be placed in December again.

## 4. More ideas

### Launch mechanics
1. **Coordinate tease.** A week before each drop, post only the GPS coordinate. People guess the place. Reveal on
   launch day. It's the collection idea turned into a game.
2. **Wear-test at the real place.** Run there at sunrise in the samples and record the actual temperature and
   humidity. Print those real readings on the conditions label instead of estimates.
3. **Numbered pieces.** Every limited piece carries its number on the label, e.g. "DROP 001 · 047 / 150".
4. **Seven-day window.** Pre-orders or the drop stay open for seven days, then close. That cuts stock risk.
5. **Drop run.** Launch morning: a community run in KL in the new pieces, with coffee after. First buyers get there first.

### The core line
6. **Make the core the "hot and wet" product.** The research found nobody owns running in humid, rainy weather, which
   is exactly Malaysia. Build the core around fabric that dries fast and doesn't cling. That's the product proof the
   brand is still missing.
7. **Three colours only.** For example black, off-white and one Malaysian accent, so restocks are simple.
8. **Name it.** For example "Kit 000" or "OFFNORM Daily". The limited drops then become 001, 002 and so on.

### Community (from "No run is perfect")
9. **A slow run club.** 7 min/km welcome, walk breaks allowed. The research found nobody speaks to slow, ordinary
   runners. That's the clearest gap OFFNORM can own.
10. **Early access later.** Once there are regulars, give run-club members first access to drops (Bandit and
    Tracksmith both do this).
11. **Run club and café partners.** Small kits for local run clubs, meet-ups at kopitiams and cafés.

### Content (the ZERA department can make these)
12. **One short film per drop**, shot at the location, plus a "field notes" post with the weather data.
13. **Behind the scenes:** sample rounds, mistakes, fixes. It suits "No run is perfect".

### Sales
14. **Two channels.** Core on the website plus Shopee or TikTok Shop for reach. Limited drops only on the website,
    to keep them special.
15. **Never discount a limited drop.** If stock is left over, keep it for a later archive event instead of a sale.

### Returning collections
16. **The Monsoon returns every November** as THE MONSOON 02, 03 and so on. Other collections can return on their
    season once the first round is done.

## 5. Decisions the founder needs to make

1. Who makes it, and what are their minimums and lead times?
2. The price tier for core and for drops.
3. Quantity per limited piece (e.g. 100? 150? 300?).
4. Kit 000 (permanent collection): pieces and colours.
5. Drop order: The Dune first (May) or keep The Sky + The Dune together.
6. Website + marketplace, or website only.
7. Budget for the first year.
