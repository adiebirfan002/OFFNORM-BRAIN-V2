# Satisfy

Paris. Founded 2015 by Brice Partouche. Road and trail apparel, now footwear too.

Labels used in this file: **[S]** = from a source in [sources.md](sources.md). **[M]** = from memory, check before
quoting it anywhere public. **[R]** = my read of the brand, an opinion.

## The one-line idea

Running is not a sport to win. It is a way to reach "the High", and gear exists to remove whatever gets in the way.

## Origin

- Partouche started a skate brand at 16, then ran the denim label April77 for about 15 years. His world was punk,
  skateboarding and music, not athletics. **[S]**
- He started running in 2014, felt the runner's high on an early run, and found that normal running kit distracted
  him: tight, neon, chafing, nowhere for keys. Satisfy came out of that complaint. **[S]**
- He prototyped for about a year in a Paris atelier before going to factories, and still tests pieces himself on
  runs along the Canal Saint-Martin. **[S]**

## Narrative

| Piece | What Satisfy says | 
|---|---|
| Enemy | Performance culture: times, podiums, neon, the idea that a runner is only a runner |
| Belief | The high needs body and mind in harmony, so remove distraction first |
| Name for it | "Technologies of peace and silence" **[S]** |
| Self-description | "Romantic approach to performance"; "anti-running running brand"; "a cult brand" **[S]** |
| Community name | "Running Cult Member"; the wider world is "The Running Cult" **[S]** |
| Customer word | Customers are called "guests" **[S]** |
| Comparison they use | A skateboard brand for people who love the outdoors. No score, no winner **[S]** |

The founder's line that sums up the audience: runners aren't just runners. The brand is built for the rest of the
person: the music, the clothes, the taste. **[S]**

## Product as proof

Satisfy never claims a mood without a fabric to back it. Every idea has a named material: **[S]**

- **MothTech**: washed cotton tee with moth-style holes mapped to where the body needs air. Idea borrowed from
  worn-out vintage band tees. This is the most important piece for OFFNORM to study: it makes *cotton* a running
  technology by adding ventilation and a story.
- **Justice**: in-house synthetic with a silk feel. Story attached: French silk mills lost their work overseas, and
  Satisfy uses that weaving skill for technical cloth.
- **GhostFleece, CoffeeThermal, CloudMerino, AuraLite, PeaceShell, Rippy**: every fabric gets a name with a
  capital in the middle. **[S]** for the first three, **[M]** for the rest.
- Uses suppliers shared with luxury houses (Schoeller is named). **[S]**

Signature silhouette they claim to have started: bandana at the neck, cropped or fringed tee, short shorts. They
call it "the Satisfy silhouette" and are happy when it spreads without credit. **[S]**

## Visual identity

- **Logo**: a plain wordmark, SATISFY in capitals, custom-drawn rather than a released font. No symbol, no
  running figure. Unofficial write-ups disagree on whether it is clean or heavy, so look at the garments yourself
  before deciding. **[S, unofficial]**
- **Colour**: washed, sun-faded, aged. Black, white, off-white, earth and bleached tones, tonal tie-dye. Almost
  never neon. **[S]**
- **Graphics**: band-merch logic. Slogans, tour-tee layouts, back prints, care labels and warning labels used as
  decoration, sewn-on patches. **[M]**
- **Photo and film**: overexposed, dreamy, grainy. City people in desert and mountain. Films scored like indie
  records. Feed has no motivational quotes. **[S]**

## Voice

Romantic, short, a little mystical. Talks about feeling, silence, the high. Never talks about pace. The founder's
own voice carries most of it. **[S]**

## How it spreads

- **POSSESSED** magazine from 2021, online then print: the brand publishes culture, not catalogues. **[S]**
- At UTMB it rented a chalet ("Chamonix Off-Road Supply") and hosted runs and workshops instead of a stand. **[S]**
- "Re-Possessed" at the Paris Marathon: runners make their own singlet from leftover cloth. **[S]**
- The online shop is described as a merch table: you buy a souvenir of the experience. **[S]**
- Since about 2025 the content has moved to real race reports and runner stories; product is designed to support a
  story, not the other way round. **[S]**
- Collaborators: Hoka, Leki, Maurten, Hydrapak on the performance side; Crocs, Aesop, Sonic Youth on the culture
  side; Levi's and adidas reported for 2026. **[S]**
- Athletes are picked for being interesting people who run. **[S]**

## Business facts

- Prices (2024 reporting): shorts about US$240, tees about US$140, hydration vest about US$310. **[S]**
- Series B of €11m reported, led by a Chanel heir. **[S]**
- Own shoe, TheROCKER, launched 2025. **[M]**
- Bigger brands have copied the distressed tee; the founder has complained publicly. **[S]**

## What OFFNORM can take

1. **Cotton can be technical if you engineer it and name it.** MothTech is the proof. OFFNORM is a cotton brand,
   so it needs its own named cotton idea, not Satisfy's holes.
2. **Name the feeling you sell.** Satisfy owns "the High". OFFNORM needs one word of its own.
3. **Name the people.** "Running Cult Member" turns a customer into a member.
4. **A plain wordmark is enough when everything around it has character.** The labels, slogans and fabric names do
   the work.
5. **Publish culture.** A zine, a film, a race report, before a product page.

## What not to copy

Moth holes, the word "cult", "the High", the bandana-and-crop silhouette, the sun-faded desert look. Each of these
is already read as Satisfy, and the founder calls out copies in public.
