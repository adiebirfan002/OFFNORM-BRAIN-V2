# What the four brands have in common

Read the four studies first: [satisfy](satisfy.md), [acg](acg.md), [fpa](fpa.md), [norda](norda.md).
Everything in this file is my reading across them.

## Seven patterns

**1. Each one has an enemy.** Three of the four are clear about what they are against, and the fourth (FPA) has
the weakest story. A brand called OFFNORM has to say which norm.

**2. Each one owns one word.** High. Conditions. Archive. Dyneema. Not a sentence, a word. Everything else hangs
from it.

**3. The story is proved by a thing you can touch.** A moth hole, a fibre, a K2 photograph. None of them asks
you to believe a mood on its own.

**4. None of the logos shows running.** No figure, no wing, no swoosh-like stroke, no shoe. All four are letters.
Two are plain wordmarks, two are three-letter marks. The character comes from the system around the logo:
labels, numbers, fabric names, small text, ®/™.

**5. The founder was the first customer.** Partouche could not find kit he would wear. The Martires could not
find a shoe they liked. Climbers posted their shoes back to Nike. The story starts with a real annoyance.

**6. Place is in the product.** Paris silk mills. Utah rock colours. Chaozhou brick. Quebec winters. None of them
is from "everywhere".

**7. Naming is a system.** Fabrics, products and drops are named by one rule each, and the rule is part of the
identity.

## Where the gaps are

- **Climate.** All four design for cold, dry or temperate places. Nobody owns hot and wet. Most of the world's
  new runners live in exactly that weather.
- **Humour in premium running.** ACG has it, but ACG is an outdoor brand owned by Nike. Among independent running
  labels the mood is romantic or solemn.
- **The slow and the ordinary.** Satisfy rejects performance but still sells desert ultras and beautiful people.
  Nobody is speaking for the 7 min/km runner who walked the last two kilometres.
- **Southeast Asia.** FPA shows an Asian label can build this kind of system. None exists from Malaysia. The
  local scene is busy (run clubs, cafés, a full race calendar) and dressed by global brands.
- **Price.** Three of the four are far out of reach for most Malaysian runners. There is room for the same level
  of thought at a local price.

## What this means for OFFNORM

OFFNORM already has three of the seven: a name with a stance, a founder with a real view, and a belief line
("No run is perfect."). It is missing a named enemy, an owned word, a product proof, a naming system and a
decided logo. Those are worked through in [../brand/narrative.md](../brand/narrative.md) and
[../brand/logo-brief.md](../brand/logo-brief.md).
