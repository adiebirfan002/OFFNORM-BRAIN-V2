# norda

Montreal and the Eastern Townships, Quebec. Founded 2020, first shoe 2021. Trail running footwear.

Labels: **[S]** sourced, **[M]** from memory (verify), **[R]** my read. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

## The one-line idea

The world does not need another trail shoe. It needs a better one. So make one thing, from the best material
there is, and say very little.

## Origin

- Nick and Willa Martire, a married couple who ran trails together for years and both worked in commercial
  footwear. Willa's complaint was personal before it was commercial: trail shoes were heavy and stiff. **[S]**
- In early 2020 they used their industry contacts to reach athletes, craftspeople and material specialists.
  Louis-Martin Tremblay (design) and Gerard Cleal (brand) joined as partners. **[S]**
- Nick refused to launch without a real technical reason to exist. It took about two years to develop Dyneema
  the way they wanted; only then did the brand's identity settle. **[S]**
- Tested in Canadian conditions from -35°C to +35°C. **[S]**
- Sources disagree on the founding year (2020 or 2021); the first shoe, the 001, came out in 2021. **[S]**

## Narrative

| Piece | What norda says |
|---|---|
| Enemy | Mediocrity, copycat product, shoes that wear out |
| Belief | Less is more. Make the finest thing, ignore trends and competitors **[S]** |
| Proof | Dyneema upper, described as the strongest, lightest fibre. Vibram sole **[S]**, **[M]** for Vibram |
| Sustainability | Durability. The reported line is that true sustainability is found in durability **[S, unofficial]** |
| Slogans | "Designed for runners. By runners." and "one giant leap for trail-kind" **[S]** |
| Place | Proudly Canadian, with makers in Italy, the Netherlands and China named openly **[S]** |

**[R]** norda's story is the simplest of the four: a material. Everything else is built to stay out of its way.

## Visual identity

- **Logo**: the word norda, always lowercase, always with ™. Quiet geometric sans. No symbol. The ™ is treated
  as part of the mark, not a legal afterthought. **[S, unofficial]** for lowercase; **[M]** for the rest.
- **Name**: probably "northern". "Norda" is the adjective of "nordo", north, in Esperanto. The brand's own
  founder page does not explain it, so this is an inference. **[R]**
- **Colour**: mineral and alpine. Near-black, glacier grey, moss, off-white, a pale stone pink. The white
  undyed look of the first shoe came from the fibre itself. **[S, unofficial]**, **[M]**
- **Naming**: numbers. 001, 002, 003, 005. A product line that reads like a lab register. **[M]**
- **Brand on product**: the wordmark is set large and plain on the shoe. The fabric's own texture is the graphic.

## Voice

Calm, exact, a little stubborn. Talks about material, kilometres and craft. No hype, no slang, no jokes. **[S]**

## How it spreads

- One hero product for a long time before a second.
- High price as a signal: roughly US$285 and up. **[M]**
- Collaborations with quality-led names outside running (Zegna, Reigning Champ, Ciele). **[M]**
- Stocked by fashion retailers as well as run shops; covered by Mr Porter as a cult sneaker. **[S]**

## What OFFNORM can take

1. **One material truth.** norda is Dyneema. OFFNORM says cotton, so cotton must be the hero and be explained as
   carefully as norda explains its fibre: which cotton, what weight, what knit, why it works when you sweat.
2. **Do not launch a story without the product reason.** norda waited two years for its proof.
3. **Number the products.** OFN-001, OFN-002. Cheap, clean, instantly serious.
4. **Make the legal mark part of the logo.** The ™ and ® do real visual work on a plain wordmark.
5. **Say where it is from and where it is made**, plainly. Designed in Malaysia. Made in (the true place).
6. **Restraint.** A new brand looks more expensive by doing less.

## What not to copy

Lowercase geometric wordmark with ™, the all-white undyed look, the mineral palette. And the silence: norda can
be quiet because its material is unique. A cotton tee is not unique, so OFFNORM needs more voice than norda has.
